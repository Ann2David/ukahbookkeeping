You find the opportunity.

Build relationships with businesses that need stronger accounting and finance support. UKAH handles the technical assessment, proposal, onboarding and service delivery while you focus on prospecting and developing opportunities.

The Opportunity

Not a one-time referral. A sales partnership.

UKAH is building a network of independent sales professionals and business-development individuals who can identify, qualify and develop potential clients using their own networks, prospecting skills and relationships.

Prospect

Use your network, relationships and prospecting methods to identify businesses that need financial support.

Develop

Build the relationship, understand the prospect's needs and develop a qualified opportunity.

Partner

UKAH handles the accounting expertise and service delivery while you continue to develop business opportunities.

Who We're Looking For

Built for people who know how to sell.

This opportunity is particularly suited to people comfortable with prospecting, relationship-building, follow-up and business development.

Sales professionals

People with a track record or practical experience in selling services and building client relationships.

Business-development professionals

Independent professionals who can identify opportunities and develop them through a sales process.

Connected professionals

People with meaningful access to business owners or decision-makers in our target markets.
