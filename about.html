Who We Are

At Ukah Bookkeeping Services, we help Ontario freelancers, sole proprietors, and small businesses transform messy financial records into clear, tax-ready numbers. We believe that running a business is hard enough without the added stress of fighting spreadsheets, managing payroll, and untangling tax deadlines.
As specialized QuickBooks Online professionals, we clean up historical backlog, build automated workflows that run in the background, and seamlessly handle your ongoing HST compliance and year-end T1 tax filings.
We deliver organized records and efficient systems so you can make decisions with total confidence minus the confusion.
