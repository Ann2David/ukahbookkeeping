Three Ways to Work With Us

Pick the level of ownership that fits your business today.

Each path solves a different problem. You can start with one and move to another as your business grows.

01

Fully Managed Accounting

We place a dedicated accounting professional inside your business on-site or remote. You direct the work. We remain the legal employer PAYE, pension, NSITF and every other statutory obligation sit with us, not you.

Best for businesses that want the function handled, without adding headcount to manage.
  • Daily accounting operations reconciliations, payables & receivables
  • Payroll processing plus PAYE, pension, and other statutory remittances
  • Structured month-end close and standardized monthly reporting
  • Ongoing supervision and quality control with continuity if your resource is out
02

Direct Hire Recruitment

We recruit, screen, and place a vetted accounting or finance professional who becomes your employee from day one a permanent hire, without you running the search, interviews, or vetting yourself.

Best for businesses ready to own the hire, but want it done right the first time.
03

Build Your Finance Team

One person isn't enough forever. As you grow, we help you plan and build a complete in-house finance function combining outsourced support and direct hires as your needs evolve, so your team grows with intention instead of in a scramble.

Best for businesses scaling past their first finance hire and ready to build real internal capability.
