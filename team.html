About Chidinma

Reading the Story Behind the Numbers

Chidinma Ukah is the founder of Ukah Bookkeeping Services, an Ontario-based firm that helps small businesses build strong financial foundations through accurate bookkeeping, tax compliance, and financial clarity. With over a decade experience in accounting, bookkeeping, finance, and business operations, she brings a practical perspective that helps business owners make informed decisions and grow with confidence.


She holds an MSc in Economics, an AAT qualification, and is a QuickBooks Online Advanced ProAdvisor. Combining technical expertise with strategic insight, Chidinma leverages cloud-based accounting solutions, including QuickBooks Online, Xero, and Sage, to deliver reliable bookkeeping services tailored to the needs of modern businesses.


Chidinma is particularly passionate about supporting SME entrepreneurs who are establishing or expanding their businesses in Ontario. She helps founders navigate the Canadian financial landscape through organized bookkeeping, T1 tax preparation for sole proprietors, and HST/GST compliance, enabling them to meet their CRA obligations while building sustainable businesses in Canada

Accurate bookkeeping, practical reporting, and dependable financial systems for small businesses.
